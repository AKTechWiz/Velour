Design Iterations & Solutions

Signature Features Built for VELOUR

Minimalist Navigation

Left-Aligned 3-Line Menu

Replaced crowded top navigation links with a high-fashion, minimalist 3-line drawer trigger positioned comfortably on the left with generous borders. Hover or click unveils the full couture catalog categorized with Cormorant Garamond typography.

  • Zero clutter: Hero imagery and brand mark take center stage
  • Slide-over drawer with instant access to Festive, Pret, Men, & Unstitched
  • Quick direct links for Currency switcher (PKR / USD / GBP / AED / SAR)
View Full Resolution
Layout Balancing

Symmetrical 6-Pillar Categories

Addressed irregular image height discrepancies by enforcing a golden ratio editorial grid. Every category pillar (Festive, Unstitched, Pret, Men, Accessories, Outerwear) renders with equal visual weight, subtle hover zoom, and champagne gold borders.

  • Equalized aspect ratios preventing layout shifts
  • Strict department isolation: zero cross-category product leakage
  • Adapts from 3-column desktop to 2-column tablet to 1-column mobile
View Category Grid
Editorial Commerce

Multi-Product "Shop The Look"

Engineered an editorial ensemble showcase featuring a full Pakistani bridal look. Interactive pulsating gold hotspots allow customers to discover and purchase individual items—Crimson Peshwas, Embroidered Dupatta, and Velvet Khussa—directly from one coordinated visual.

  • Pulsating radar hotspots positioned over authentic ensemble garments
  • Instant quick-add drawer with individual size selections
  • Catalog sync: All ensemble products appear in their native departments
View Ensemble Mockup
Visual Storytelling

100% Paired Craftsmanship Hovers

Eliminated generic image swaps. Hovering on any product card smoothly cross-fades into an authentic, macro detail shot of that exact garment—highlighting hand-embroidered dabka, zardozi needlework, velvet pile depth, and silk weave textures.

  • Zero mismatched hover pictures across 24 full-catalog items
  • Optimized Next.js Image caching with high-priority lazy loading
  • Instant visual proof of bespoke couture quality
